Key terms

Withdrawal

Water taken from a river, lake or aquifer. Much of it flows back.

Consumption

Water that doesn’t come back: it evaporates or ends up in a product.

Water stress

When demand nears a region’s renewable supply. It’s always local.

Water footprint

All the water used to make a product, across its supply chain.

How business is tackling it

Fixing data centers

Microsoft

Planet · Profit

Closed-loop chip cooling in all new designs since 2024: ~125M liters saved per site each year

Amazon

People

First disclosure: 2.5B gallons in 2025; says about ⅔ is returned to local communities

Fixing the real problem

N-Drip

People · Planet

Gravity-powered drip irrigation for small farms: 57% less water in an IRRI rice trial

Levi Strauss & Co.

Profit · Planet

Water<Less finishing uses up to 96% less water; 3B+ liters saved so far

My take

Tech has the worst headlines but moves fastest. Farms need the most help.

What businesses should do

People

Build where water is plentiful, and invest in the local systems you draw from.

Profit

Price water risk in. Droughts shut plants, and leaks waste $39B a year.

Planet

Go after the 70%: fund efficient irrigation with farm suppliers, and reuse water.

Water is a local problem with global supply chains.
