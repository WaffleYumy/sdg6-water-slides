01 · The basics

Four words that change the story

Withdrawal

Water taken from a river, lake or aquifer. Much of it flows back.

Consumption

Water that never comes back: it evaporates or goes into a product.

Water stress

Demand close to a region’s renewable supply. Always local.

Water footprint

All the water behind a product. One cotton T-shirt: about 2,700 liters.

02 · The AI myth

AI and water: how bad is it, really?

Poll answer: C

A full year of US data-center water ≈ 5.6 hours of US farm irrigation.

17B gal/yr direct (LBNL, 2023) ÷ 73B gal/day consumed by irrigation (USGS, 2015)

Add the power plants that run them
≈ 3 days. Still small, nationally.

Real downsides
  • Clustered in dry towns
  • Power-plant water ≈ 12×
  • Could grow 2–4× by 2028
Real upsides
  • Closed-loop cooling: ~0 water
  • Efficiency rising fast
  • Tax revenue for towns

05 · What to do

Three moves for any business

01

People

Build where water is plentiful, and invest in the local systems you draw from.

02

Profit

Price water risk in. Droughts shut plants, and leaks waste $39B a year.

03

Planet

Go after the 71%: fund efficient irrigation with farm suppliers, and reuse water.

Water is a local problem with global supply chains.
